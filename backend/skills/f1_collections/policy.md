# Policy: F1 Collections

## Cadence rungs
| Rung | Trigger | Channel | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | T-3 days | WhatsApp template | soft reminder |
| 2 | due date | statement + WhatsApp | neutral |
| 3 | overdue | escalation email | firm |
| 4 | long overdue | stop-supply RECOMMENDATION | Branch Head decides |

## Exclusions
- Disputed accounts (always)
- Accounts with a promise_record inside its grace window

## Language rules
- Tamil / Malayalam / English at parity of meaning; match the
  dealer's CRM language preference.
